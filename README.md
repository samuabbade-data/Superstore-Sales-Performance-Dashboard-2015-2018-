# Superstore-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-2015-2018-
Sales perfomance
## Questions to answer:

1. Which product sub-categories drive the majority of store revenue?
2. How does shipping method performance vary across geographic regions?
3. Which customer segment produces the highest business value?

---

## Hypotheses

1. High-ticket items like Technology and Furniture generate the vast majority of total sales.
2. Standard Class shipping dominates revenue across all regions due to lower cost for consumers.
3. Individual Consumers generate the highest overall sales volume, but Corporate and Home Office buyers yield higher Average Order Values (AOV).

---

## Findings & Answers

### 1. Product Revenue Leaders

* **Top Performers:** **Phones** and **Chairs** serve as primary revenue drivers for the store.
* **Low Performers:** Items such as **Fasteners** and **Labels** contribute negligible top-line sales.

### 2. Regional & Logistics Performance

* **Top Regions:** The **East** and **West** regions lead in overall sales volume.
* **Best Channel:** **Standard Class** shipping accounts for over 60% of total revenue uniformly across all regions.
* **Underutilized Channel:** **Same Day** delivery represents the smallest revenue fraction in every region.

### 3. Customer Segment Value

* **Most Valuable by Volume:** The **Consumer** segment leads total revenue (~$1.15M total sales).
* **Most Valuable by Order Size:** The **Home Office** segment delivers the highest Average Order Value ($475.37 per order), outperforming Corporate ($461.77) and Consumer (~$452.53).
